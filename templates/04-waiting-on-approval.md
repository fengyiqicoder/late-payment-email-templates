# "It's waiting on approval" / "AP runs at the end of the month"

The person you deal with often can't pay you. Get the name of whoever can, and the exact step the invoice is stuck at.

## Friendly

**Subject:** Re: invoice {number}

```text
Thanks {name}, understood.

Who is it waiting on, and is there anything I can send to help it through, like a W-9 or PO number? If it's easier, I can email accounts payable directly.

{your_name}
```

## Firm

**Subject:** Invoice {number} - approval status

```text
Hi {name},

Invoice {number} has been waiting on approval since {date_1}. It's now {days_late} days past the {due_date} due date we agreed.

Could you give me the name and email of the approver, or a date it will be approved by? I'd like to get it into this month's payment run.

{your_name}
```

## Final

**Subject:** Invoice {number}: please escalate

```text
Hi {name},

Approval for invoice {number} has taken {days_waiting} days. Our agreement was payment within {terms} days of the invoice.

Please escalate this so the {amount} is paid by {deadline}. If it isn't, {next_step}.

{your_name}
```

---

Swap every `{placeholder}`. `{next_step}` is the consequence you'll actually follow through on: stop work, add the late fee from our contract, or send it to small claims court. Don't threaten anything you won't do.
