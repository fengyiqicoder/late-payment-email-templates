# "We're not happy with the work"

A complaint that arrives only after the invoice is a different problem from one that arrives with the work. Separate the parts they're disputing from the parts they aren't, and get the undisputed amount paid now.

## Friendly

**Subject:** Re: invoice {number} - your feedback

```text
Hi {name},

Sorry to hear that. Could you tell me specifically what isn't right, so I can look at it?

In the meantime, the parts that aren't in question ({undisputed_items}) come to {undisputed_amount}. Could you pay that now, and we'll settle the rest once we've sorted out the issue?

{your_name}
```

## Firm

**Subject:** Invoice {number} - delivered work

```text
Hi {name},

The work on invoice {number} was delivered on {delivery_date} and approved on {approval_date} ({approval_reference}). This is the first I've heard of a problem with it.

I'm happy to fix anything that doesn't match what we agreed in {scope_reference}. Please list the specific issues by {date_1}. The {amount} remains due.

{your_name}
```

## Final

**Subject:** Invoice {number}: outstanding balance

```text
Hi {name},

I asked on {date_1} for the specific issues with the work on invoice {number} and haven't received them. The work was delivered and approved as described in my last email.

Please pay the {amount} by {deadline}. If you still believe something is wrong, send the details before then and I'll respond within two business days. Otherwise, {next_step}.

{your_name}
```

---

Swap every `{placeholder}`. `{next_step}` is the consequence you'll actually follow through on: stop work, add the late fee from our contract, or send it to small claims court. Don't threaten anything you won't do.
