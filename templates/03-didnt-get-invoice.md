# "We never got the invoice"

Sometimes true, sometimes a stall. Either way, resend immediately, make it impossible to lose again, and keep the original due date.

## Friendly

**Subject:** Invoice {number} (resent)

```text
Hi {name},

Sorry about that. Invoice {number} for {amount} is attached again. It was first sent on {sent_date} with a due date of {due_date}.

Could you confirm it's reached the right person this time? If invoices should go to a different address, let me know and I'll use it from now on.

{your_name}
```

## Firm (they say it again)

**Subject:** Invoice {number} - sent to accounts

```text
Hi {name},

I've now sent invoice {number} on {sent_date} and {date_2}. To make sure it doesn't go missing again, I've copied {ap_email} on this email and attached it once more.

The due date was {due_date}, so it's {days_late} days overdue. When can I expect payment?

{your_name}
```

## Final

**Subject:** Invoice {number}: confirmation needed

```text
Hi {name},

Invoice {number} has been sent three times, most recently on {date_2}. Please reply to confirm you have it and give me a payment date by {deadline}.

If I don't hear back, I'll assume it's been received and {next_step}.

{your_name}
```

---

Swap every `{placeholder}`. `{next_step}` is the consequence you'll actually follow through on: stop work, add the late fee from our contract, or send it to small claims court. Don't threaten anything you won't do.

Don't want to rewrite these by hand? Cc **getpaid@onetagai.com** on your next reminder. Each time the client replies, you get the next email written around what they said.
