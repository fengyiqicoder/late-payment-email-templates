# "Cash is tight, can we pay later or split it?"

A client who tells you this is still talking to you, which is good. Agree to a written plan with dates rather than an open-ended delay.

## Friendly

**Subject:** Re: invoice {number} - payment plan

```text
Hi {name},

Thanks for being upfront. I can split invoice {number} ({amount}) into {installments} payments:

- {amount_1} by {date_1}
- {amount_2} by {date_2}

If that works, reply "agreed" and I'll send updated invoices. If not, tell me what's realistic.

{your_name}
```

## Firm (a plan payment was missed)

**Subject:** Invoice {number}: missed installment

```text
Hi {name},

The {amount_1} installment we agreed for {date_1} hasn't arrived. The remaining balance on invoice {number} is {balance}.

Can you send it by {deadline}? If something has changed, let's talk now rather than later.

{your_name}
```

## Final

**Subject:** Invoice {number}: balance due

```text
Hi {name},

We agreed a payment plan for invoice {number} on {plan_date}, and {missed_count} installments have now been missed. The full balance of {balance} is due by {deadline}.

After that date, {next_step}.

{your_name}
```

---

Swap every `{placeholder}`. `{next_step}` is the consequence you'll actually follow through on: stop work, add the late fee from our contract, or send it to small claims court. Don't threaten anything you won't do.
