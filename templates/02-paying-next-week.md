# "We'll pay next week"

A vague promise is the most common stall. Turn it into a date they've agreed to in writing, then hold them to it.

## Friendly

**Subject:** Re: invoice {number}

```text
Thanks {name}, that works.

So I can plan, can you confirm payment will reach me by {specific_date}? Just a yes is fine.

{your_name}
```

## Firm (the week passed)

**Subject:** Re: invoice {number} - payment date

```text
Hi {name},

You mentioned payment would go out last week, but I haven't received it yet. Invoice {number} for {amount} is now {days_late} days overdue.

Can you check whether it was sent, and if not, the date it will be?

{your_name}
```

## Final (second missed promise)

**Subject:** Invoice {number}: second missed date

```text
Hi {name},

Payment for invoice {number} was promised for {date_1} and again for {date_2}. It still hasn't arrived and the invoice is {days_late} days overdue.

I need the full {amount} by {deadline}. After that, {next_step}.

{your_name}
```

---

Swap every `{placeholder}`. `{next_step}` is the consequence you'll actually follow through on: stop work, add the late fee from our contract, or send it to small claims court. Don't threaten anything you won't do.
