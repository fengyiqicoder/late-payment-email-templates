# They haven't replied at all

Silence usually means the email got buried or went to someone who can't approve payment. Each message here is shorter than the last and adds one new piece of information so it's easy to answer.

## Friendly (3 to 7 days late)

**Subject:** Invoice {number} - quick check

```text
Hi {name},

Just checking that invoice {number} for {amount} reached you. It was due on {due_date}. I've attached it again in case it got lost.

If it's already in your payment run, ignore this.

Thanks,
{your_name}
```

## Firm (2 to 3 weeks late)

**Subject:** Invoice {number} is {days_late} days overdue

```text
Hi {name},

Invoice {number} for {amount} was due on {due_date} and is now {days_late} days overdue. I haven't heard back on my last two emails.

Could you tell me when it will be paid? If someone else handles invoices on your side, send me their email and I'll follow up with them directly.

{your_name}
```

## Final (30+ days late)

**Subject:** Final notice: invoice {number}

```text
Hi {name},

Invoice {number} for {amount} is now {days_late} days overdue. I've written on {date_1} and {date_2} without a reply.

If payment isn't received by {deadline}, I'll pause any remaining work and {next_step}.

I'd rather sort this out directly, so a one-line reply with a payment date is all I need.

{your_name}
```

---

Swap every `{placeholder}`. `{next_step}` is the consequence you'll actually follow through on: stop work, add the late fee from our contract, or send it to small claims court. Don't threaten anything you won't do.
